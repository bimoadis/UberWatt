Looking ahead

This part is a bet on where flexible load goes next, not a product available today. We label it that way on purpose.

Future outlook — not a current market

Quantum engineering inverts the problem, and that makes it interesting.

A mining rig can stop in seconds and lose nothing. A quantum system cannot. Dilution refrigerators take days to reach operating temperature, and a power interruption destroys the run. Today these facilities draw tens of kilowatts, not megawatts — small enough to ignore. If error-corrected machines scale, that changes.

The signal they need is the opposite of a miner's. Not "when does the window open" but "where is the surplus permanent." That is a siting question, and it is answered with the same data — read across years instead of minutes.

Miners and AI clusters — today

  • Stop and start in seconds
  • Want the next ninety minutes
  • Profit from volatility
  • Already the largest flexible load on ERCOT

Quantum facilities — the bet

  • Cannot be power-cycled at all
  • Want the next ten years
  • Pay a premium for stability
  • Would site where surplus is chronic, not occasional

Same archive, different question. That is the reason the historical record is worth building now, before anyone needs it.
